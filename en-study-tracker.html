    Focus with a timer, get a study log for free

    Most study trackers ask you to start a log before you begin and fill it in when you finish — exactly the step that gets skipped on a tired evening. TimerTrain turns that around: the focus timer is the tracker. Sign in with Google (free) and every minute the timer actually runs is logged. Paused time is not counted, so the total is real study, not time the tab was open.

    This page loads a two-round session: Reading 25, Break 5, Maths 25, Break 5. Each timer's name becomes the label of the log, so naming blocks by subject is all it takes to see later how your time split between subjects.

    Three ways to look back

    • Log — one bar per day, newest first. Tap a day to open its timeline: what you did, from when, for how long.
    • Calendar — a month at a glance, with circles or bars sized by how much you studied. Filter by subject to see, say, every day you practised Spanish this month.
    • Summary — totals for 7 or 30 days and a pie chart by subject.

    When you stop the timer you can leave a one-line note ("100 vocabulary cards", "past paper 3"); it appears in the log and the calendar. Forgot to start the timer? Use "+ Add past time" for today or yesterday.

    Why logging helps you keep going

    A visible record of past effort is one of the strongest reasons to show up again. The home screen shows today's log and your streak 🔥, so even a ten-minute session keeps the chain alive and colours in the calendar. Aim for "no blank days" rather than long sessions, and let the calendar do the motivating.

    FAQ

    Does it record my study time automatically?
    Yes. When you are signed in (free, with Google), the time the timer actually runs is logged automatically and kept for good. Paused time is not counted.

    Can I track time per subject?
    Yes. Each timer's name becomes a label, and the Data view shows totals and shares per label. Labels can be given one of 16 colours.

    Do I need to install an app?
    No. It runs in any browser on phone, tablet or computer. Sign in with the same Google account and your log is the same on every device. You can also add it to your home screen.

    Can anyone else see my log?
    No. Only your own account can read or write your records. A share link shares the timer setup (lengths and names), never your log.
