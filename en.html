    What is TimerTrain?

    TimerTrain is a free online timer that lets you chain multiple timers together — like train cars — and play them in sequence, over and over. No install, no sign-up. Open the page, press play, and use it as a sequential timer, interval timer, or repeating multi-timer right away.

    How to use it

    Tap "Add timer" to line up as many timers as you need, tap the time to edit it, and give each one a name and a color. Set how many rounds to repeat and press start — your work/break or exercise/rest cycle keeps running automatically. Share your exact setup with anyone by simply copying the URL.

    FAQ

    What is TimerTrain?
    TimerTrain is a free online sequential timer. You line up several timers and it runs them one after another, switching automatically, for as many rounds as you set (or endlessly).

    How do I run multiple timers in a row?
    Tap "Add timer" to line up as many timers as you need, set a time, name and color for each, then press play. A sound plays each time one ends and the next timer starts by itself.

    Can I save or share my timer setup?
    Yes. Your whole setup lives in the URL. Use the share button to copy the link — bookmark it to save, or send it so someone else opens the exact same timers. With a free sign-in you can also save it to “My timers” under a name and open it from your phone or computer.

    Is it free? Do I need an account or an app?
    It is free, with no sign-up and nothing to install. It runs in the browser on phone, tablet and desktop, and you can add it to your home screen to use it like an app. If you want saved timers, sync between devices and a usage history, you can sign in for free with Google — only then.

    Can I track how much time I study or work?
    Yes. When you use the timer while signed in (free), the time it runs is recorded automatically, and your account page shows the last 7 days: total time, sessions and a daily chart. Paused time is not counted.

    Does it stay accurate in a background tab?
    Yes. Remaining time is calculated from the clock rather than counted tick by tick, so switching tabs does not make it drift. While it runs the screen is kept awake, and sounds mark every switch and the finish.
