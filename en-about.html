About TimerTrain

TimerTrain was made for the moments when you want several timers to run one after another — study then break, work then rest. There is nothing to sign up for or install; it runs in the browser on your phone or computer. Your whole setup lives in the page URL, so it is easy to bookmark or share. It is available in English, Japanese and Spanish.

What we care about

  • Every core timer feature is free and works without an account
  • Signing in is optional. Sign in with Google only if you want saved "My timers", sync between phone and computer, and a 7-day usage history
  • There are currently no ads on TimerTrain
  • We never handle passwords, and saved data can be read and written only by the signed-in owner (see the Privacy Policy)
  • You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from the account page

How it is built

TimerTrain is developed by one person, with the help of AI assistants for design and coding. Behaviour, security and data handling are checked before each release, and the service keeps improving based on feedback.

Bugs and requests
