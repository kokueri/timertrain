    What is HIIT?

    HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training) alternates hard bursts of exercise with short rests. Because it loads both your heart and your muscles in a short session, it's a popular way for busy people to build an exercise habit. Common work-to-rest ratios are 2:1 (for example 40s + 20s) and 1:1 (30s + 30s).

    Example workout

    Beginners can start with 30 seconds of work and 30 seconds of rest for 6 sets. As it gets easier, lengthen the work interval or add sets, and finish with a one-minute cool-down timer.
