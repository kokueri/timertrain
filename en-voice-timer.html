    Say it, and your timer chain is ready

    Open "Timer settings", tap "🎤 Say or type it", and say (or type) the timers you want. Names, lengths and the number of rounds are set in one go. You see the timers before they are added, and you can undo afterwards.

    🎤 Try building a timer by voice

    Things you can say

    • "Study 25 min, break 5 min, 3 times": Study 25 min → Break 5 min, repeated 3 times
    • "Plank 30 sec rest 10 sec 8 times": No commas needed — great for workouts
    • "Warm up 5 min, then work 25 min break 5 min x 4, finally review 10 min": Warm up → (work, break) × 4 → review, in one chain
    • "Work 25 min break 5 min forever": Repeats until you stop it

    How to use it

    1. Tap "🎤 Say or type it" inside the "Timer settings" button.
    2. Tap 🎤 next to the box and speak (or just type).
    3. Check the preview and tap "Use this". Then press start.

    FAQ

    Which browsers support voice input?

    The 🎤 button appears in browsers that support voice input (the Web Speech API), such as Chrome, Edge and Safari. In other browsers you can type the same sentence into the box and get the same timers.

    Do I need commas or pauses?

    No. "study 25 min break 5 min 3 times" works as well as a sentence with commas — it splits wherever a new name follows a time.

    What does "3 times x 2" do?

    The repeats are multiplied, so you get 6 rounds.

    Is my voice or text sent anywhere?

    Turning the text into timers happens on your device and nothing is sent. Turning speech into text is done by your browser; in Chrome, Google's servers convert the speech.

    Can I save the timer I made?

    Copy the share link to save or share the whole setup as a URL. With a free account you can also save it as a named "My timer".
