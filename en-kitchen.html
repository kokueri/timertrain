    Cooking is where multiple timers shine

    When several dishes are on the go at once, keeping every time in your head is hard. With TimerTrain you can line up steps like "main 15 min → side 10 min → finish 5 min" and time them one after another, turning the steps of your recipe straight into a timer. Add a name and color to each and you'll know at a glance which dish is done when it rings.

    How to use this kitchen timer

    Three timers — main, side, and finish — are ready to go. Tap a time to match your own recipe, and add steps if you need them. Save your go-to times (pasta 9 min, soft-boiled egg 7 min, rest 10 min) and bookmark the URL to call them up in one tap next time.

    Beeps mean you don't have to watch the screen

    Each timer beeps in its final 3 seconds and signals every switch with a sound, so wet hands or a spot at the stove are no problem. It runs on phone or computer — just open the browser.
