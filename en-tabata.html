    What is Tabata training?

    Tabata is a form of high-intensity interval training: 20 seconds of all-out effort followed by 10 seconds of rest, repeated 8 times for a total of 4 minutes. It grew out of research by Dr. Izumi Tabata and colleagues at Ritsumeikan University, and is known worldwide simply as "TABATA" — a way to train both aerobic and anaerobic capacity in a very short session.

    Train safely

    Tabata only delivers its benefits when you truly go all-out, which is also what makes it demanding. Always warm up first, and scale the number of sets to how you feel — there's no need to push through when your body says stop.
