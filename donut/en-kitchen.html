    Cooking steps in order

    This page starts with three steps: main dish 15 minutes (milk tea), side dish 10 minutes (mint chocolate) and finishing 5 minutes (vanilla). When one donut is finished, a sound plays and the next step starts automatically, so you don't have to reset a timer between steps.

    How to use it

    Rename the steps to match your recipe — "Boil pasta", "Make sauce" — and set the minutes. With flour on your hands, Space starts and pauses from a keyboard. Save the link to reuse the same recipe timing next time.

    FAQ

    Can it run several timers at the same time?
    Timers here play one after another. For dishes that cook in parallel, open the page in two tabs.

    Can I hear it from another room?
    Pick a louder sound and volume in the settings. Keep the device unmuted.
