    Pomodoro, one donut at a time

    The Pomodoro Technique alternates 25 minutes of focus with a 5-minute break. Here, a strawberry-milk donut is slowly eaten during the 25 minutes; when it's finished, a blueberry donut takes over for the break. Because you can see how much is left at a glance, there's no need to keep checking the clock.

    How to use it

    The page opens with 4 rounds of "focus 25 → break 5" ready to go. Press play and it runs all the way to the fourth break on its own. Space starts and pauses, S skips to the next timer, and focus mode fills the screen with just the donut.

    Want a different length?

    If 25 minutes feels short, try the 50 + 10 study timer, or change any length in the timer settings below.

    Keep a record (optional)

    Sign in free with Google to save your setups with a name, open them on your phone or computer, and see the time you spent on your account page. The days you keep going show up as a 🔥 streak at the top left. Everything else works without an account.

    FAQ

    Can I change the times and rounds?
    Yes. Use the timer settings below to change the focus and break lengths and how many rounds to repeat.

    What happens if I pause?
    The donut and the countdown both stop where they are. Press play again to continue.

    How is it different from a normal Pomodoro timer?
    It works the same — the difference is that the time left is a shrinking donut, not only numbers.
