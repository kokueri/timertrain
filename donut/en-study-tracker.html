    A cute timer that quietly keeps your study log

    Most study trackers ask you to start a log before you begin and fill it in when you finish — exactly the step that gets skipped on a tired evening. DonutTimer turns that around: the focus timer is the tracker. Sign in with Google (free) and every minute the timer actually runs is logged. Paused time is not counted, so the total is real study, not time the tab was open.

    This page loads two rounds of Reading 25 (strawberry milk), Break 5 (blueberry), Maths 25 (mint chocolate), Break 5. Each timer's name becomes the label of the log, so giving each subject its own flavor keeps both the timer and your records easy to tell apart.

    Looking back is part of the fun

    • Log — one bar per day, newest first. Tap a day to open its timeline: what you did, from when, for how long.
    • Calendar — a month at a glance, with circles or bars sized by how much you studied. Filter by subject to see every day you practised one thing.
    • Summary — totals for 7 or 30 days and a donut chart by subject.

    When you stop the timer you can leave a one-line note ("100 vocabulary cards", "past paper 3"); it shows up in the log and the calendar like a tiny study journal. Forgot to start the timer? Use "+ Add past time" for today or yesterday.

    No blank days

    The home screen shows today's log and your streak 🔥. Even a ten-minute session colours in the calendar and keeps the streak alive — think "one donut a day". Because the time left is the amount of donut, it also works for kids who cannot read a clock yet.

    FAQ

    Does it record my study time automatically?
    Yes. When you are signed in (free, with Google), the time the timer actually runs is logged automatically and kept for good. Paused time is not counted.

    Is the log shared with the regular TimerTrain look?
    Yes. DonutTimer is the same timer with a cute look; your log and saved timers are the same in both, and the button at the top right switches between them.

    Can I track time per subject?
    Yes. Each timer's name becomes a label, and the Data view shows totals and shares per label.

    Do I need to install an app?
    No. It runs in any browser on phone, tablet or computer, and you can add it to your home screen.
