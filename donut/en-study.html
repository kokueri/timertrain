    A calm timer for long study sessions

    This page starts with 50 minutes of study (mint chocolate) and a 10-minute break (blueberry), three times. The donut shrinks quietly as you work, so you can feel how far along you are without the pressure of ticking numbers.

    Line up your subjects

    Rename the timers to your subjects — "Math 50", "Break 10", "English 50" — and each gets its own flavor. Copy the share link to keep your study plan, or send it to a study partner so you both start on the same schedule.

    Keep a record (optional)

    Sign in free with Google to save your setups with a name, open them on your phone or computer, and see the time you spent on your account page. The days you keep going show up as a 🔥 streak at the top left. Everything else works without an account.

    FAQ

    Is 50 minutes too long?
    Change it to whatever works for you — 25 + 5 is the classic Pomodoro.

    Can I track how much I studied?
    Sign in free with Google and your study time is recorded on your account page, with a 🔥 streak for the days you keep going.
