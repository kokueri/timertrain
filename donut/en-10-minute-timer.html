    A 10 minute timer you can read at a glance

    Press play and a mint chocolate donut starts disappearing clockwise from 12 o'clock. When it's gone, time is up and an alarm sounds. You can tell how much is left from across the room without reading the numbers — though the countdown sits right in the donut hole too.

    Good for

    A ten-minute reading sprint, a quick workout, a power nap, or timing something in the oven.

    How to use it

    Press play to start, press again to pause, and use the reset button to start over. Space starts and pauses from the keyboard. Want a break after it? Press "Add timer" and the next donut starts automatically when this one is finished.

    Keep a record (optional)

    Sign in free with Google to save your setups with a name, open them on your phone or computer, and see the time you spent on your account page. The days you keep going show up as a 🔥 streak at the top left. Everything else works without an account.

    FAQ

    Does it make a sound when time is up?
    Yes. An alarm sounds when the donut is finished. You can change the sound and volume in the settings.

    Can I save it?
    Copy the share link or bookmark the page; sign in free to save it with a name.

    Is it the same as TimerTrain?
    Same timer, different look. The button at the top right opens this exact timer in TimerTrain, where the time is shown as a ring.
