    HIIT you can follow from the floor

    This page starts with 40 seconds of work and 20 seconds of rest for 8 rounds. Work is strawberry milk, rest is blueberry, and the donut shrinks as each phase runs out — big enough to read from a yoga mat or across a room.

    How to use it

    Press play and the workout runs to the end on its own, with a sound at every switch. Change the times or rounds in the timer settings, or add timers for a warm-up and cool-down. Focus mode shows only the donut, full screen.

    Keep a record (optional)

    Sign in free with Google to save your setups with a name, open them on your phone or computer, and see the time you spent on your account page. The days you keep going show up as a 🔥 streak at the top left. Everything else works without an account.

    FAQ

    Can I set different exercises?
    Yes. Add a timer for each exercise and name it — "Squats", "Push-ups", "Rest" — and they play in order.

    Will the screen turn off?
    While the timer is running, the page asks your device to keep the screen on where the browser supports it.
